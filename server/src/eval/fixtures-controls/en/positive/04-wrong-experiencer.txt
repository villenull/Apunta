Session note, dictated. John Smith, in for his fourth session. He is
settled and talks freely. His sister is the one who is not sleeping at the
moment, three weeks of broken nights with a new baby. He is not the one who is
unwell. He is here about his own worry that the worry runs in the family. His
mood is low but he is eating and sleeping. He denies any thoughts of harming
himself. He wants to know whether this is something he should watch closely.
Plan is to review again in a fortnight and to bring a list of what he notices.
