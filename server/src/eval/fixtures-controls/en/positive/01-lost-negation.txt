Session note, dictated, fifty minutes. John Smith, forty-one, in for
his eighth weekly session. He reports he still has not been sleeping. Says he
lies awake most nights from about three until the alarm. Says he has not been
able to fall back asleep after that. Describes the last two weeks as no better
at all. Denies any thoughts of harming himself. Denies hearing voices. No
change in appetite. He is working nights this month, which is new. Plan is to
keep the weekly cadence, look at the sleep window again in two weeks, and
review the diary he is keeping. He brought the diary.
