## Deviations

none. Three judgement calls are recorded here because a reviewer may want to
look at them, and none of them changes what a screen shows:

1. **`t`'s third argument.** The card writes `t(key, params)` and names `t` as
   the export, without writing a signature. `t(key, params = {}, locale =
   DEFAULT_LOCALE)` satisfies both: two arguments render the default locale
   (English, D11), and the web provider's `useI18n()` hands out `t` with its
   locale bound. Fixed decision 2 fixes the **export surface** of
   `shared/src/index.ts` at `t`, `MessageKey` and both catalogues, so no
   `createT` was added to the package — that is also why
   `web/src/lib/i18n.tsx` derives the hook's parameter type off `t` with
   `Parameters<typeof t>[1]` instead of importing a `MessageParams` it is not
   licensed to import.
2. **The two §3.6 classes, decided structurally.** Fixed decision 11 says
   symbols and sentence fragments "are not reported", and S1.4's own method for
   the fragment class leaned on the glossary. The checker decides both from the
   tree alone: a literal of nothing but symbols and punctuation is not
   reported, and a JSX text node with an interpolation or an inline element
   immediately beside it is a piece of a sentence the surrounding nodes
   complete, so it is not reported either. Every member of S1.4 §3.6's six
   fragments is caught by that rule (`days.` at `BackupCard.tsx:180`, `yet.` at
   `PlanView.tsx:304`, `A` and `, or a` at `InstructionsPanel.tsx:95`,
   `across` at `Import.tsx:187`, and `and` at the line break in
   `Workspace.tsx:345`), and no label is lost with them: `Patients`,
   `Notes`, `Loading…` and every other short string are still on the list,
   which a length-based rule would have thrown away. The consequence is the one
   the card's wording implies: a sentence JSX splits around an interpolation is
   the sentence S2.4 translates, and the checker does not police its halves.
3. **`declare const process` in `shared/src/i18n/t.ts`.** `shared` has no Node
   types in its build configuration, so the read Fixed decision 7 pins does not
   compile as written, and `shared/tsconfig.json` is not this card's to edit.
   The declaration is module-scoped, the pinned expression and its
   `typeof process !== 'undefined'` guard are used verbatim, and the emitted
   `shared/dist/i18n/t.d.ts` contains no `process` at all (checked with `grep`).
   The reasoning and the trace that shows why are in
   `docs/v2/evidence/S2.2/v4.md`.
