**2. Was the CSP header observed on a real HTML response, and with which

```
observed GET /patients: status 200, content-type "text/html; charset=utf-8",
content-security-policy "default-src 'self'; script-src 'self'; connect-src 'self';
```

## What I changed in the harness, and why it is not HS-7

**The defect.** `waitForFact`, `lastFactWhere`, `rectForLabel`, `rectSignature`
and `clickAndWaitForChange` took the child's stderr as a **string**, and every
call site passed `run.output.stderr` — evaluated **once**, at the call. A poll
loop over an immutable snapshot can only re-find what was in the buffer at the
moment it was taken. Every fact the page published after the first read was
invisible.

**The signature**, and it is what makes this checkable rather than a story:
attempt 3, and the first run of this attempt, both printed *"no
`/api/p3.4-observe` line ever appeared … (**6 marker line(s) read**)"* — the
count read from the live buffer at failure time, contradicting the sentence
beside it. A channel that carries nothing cannot report six of its lines.

**The repair**: one `stderrAt()` resolver plus thunks at the seven polling call
sites, in `scripts/v2/tauri-security.test.mjs` only. **No assertion, no
predicate, no pass condition and no timeout changed.** This makes the polls poll
— it makes the instrument able to fail, where before it could only time out.

Two smaller changes in the same file:

- the observed CSP header is now printed on a **pass** as well as a failure,
  because `check()` only surfaces its detail on a failure and this row's whole
  claim is about the directives that are really there;
- the fixture note is created **before** the observation gate, followed by one
  `ctrl+r`. A fresh sandbox data folder has no note format, so the app's first
  paint lands on **onboarding**, where no patient row exists — which is why
  attempt 3 could not click "John Smith" at all. The three HTTP calls are the
  card's own order and its own precedent; only **when** they are made changed.

A `xdotool windowfocus` hypothesis (no window manager on the bare Xvfb display)
was tested, **changed nothing, and was reverted** rather than left in as an
unexplained change.

## Unresolved items

1. **(a) FAILS, and it is very likely a card defect rather than a tree defect.**
   Observed: `tauri="undefined"`, `tauriInternals="object"`. Tauri v2
   initialises its IPC bridge in **every** webview, so `__TAURI_INTERNALS__` is
   injected regardless of configuration, and `withGlobalTauri: false` governs
   `__TAURI__` alone. C-BRIDGE@1 rule 4 forbids IPC *reachable from web
   content*, and `"capabilities": []` — asserted by V3 — is what makes it
   unreachable; the internals object existing is not itself a breach. **The
   card's (a) demands something this stack does not provide.** Recorded as a FAIL
   with the observed value, not reinterpreted, and **V3's greps were not offered
   as a substitute**. Whether (a) should assert reachability rather than the
   `typeof` of an always-injected global is a card amendment.
