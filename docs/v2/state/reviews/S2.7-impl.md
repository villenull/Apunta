## Numbered findings for the implementer

None blocking. Four observations, all minor and none changing the review's
substance:

1. **Finding 21's cross-reference is imprecise.** The evidence says
   `se eliminan` "is the word the same screen already uses elsewhere
   (`workspace.deleteBodyThirdTail`)". That key actually reads "eso lo hace y
   no **elimina** nada" — *elimina*, not *se eliminan*. The substance (the
   delete dialog uses *eliminar* vocabulary, so *se eliminan* is the right
   word for finding 21's proposal) still holds; only the quoted form is
   wrong. `web/src/routes/Workspace.tsx:775`.
2. **The C2 excerpt is internally inconsistent with C3.** C2's `tsx` scan
   reports `"voseo":0` while C3's `rg` scan, run one minute later with the
   pattern the evidence quotes, finds the one voseo hit (`format.restoreLink`,
   line 1994). The one-liner's voseo pattern must have differed from the
   quoted `rg` pattern. Finding 2 itself is correct — I reproduced the hit
   myself (R6) — so this is an evidence-quality blemish, not a missed or
   false finding.
3. **The clean-section claim about `brainstorm.contextMostRecent` is
   imprecise.** It says `contextMostRecentOne`/`contextMostRecent` "carry a
   plural map where English has none". For `contextMostRecentOne` that is
   true (English has no plural map); for `contextMostRecent` English has a
   plural map, just without the `many` category. The substance — Spanish adds
   the `many` category English lacks (AM-051) — is correct.
4. **The summary's "the two exceptions found are in group A" is loosely
   worded.** The mechanical scans actually surfaced three things, all in
   Group A: the voseo form (finding 2), the English word `entered` (finding
   1) and the two ASCII-dots keys (finding 13). The sentence is defensible
   (the two *register/translation* exceptions are findings 1 and 2) but a
   reader could count three.
