- **(b) Dirty pre-quiesce disconnect preservation/recovery gap — STILL OPEN,
  needs its own owner decision.** The proof review is explicit that `drop`
  erases a window's record and its obligations, so a dirty disconnect *before*
  a quiesce can settle `{ok:true}` over never-persisted content, that a
  reconnect does not recover it, and that closing/freezing/waiting recovers
  nothing (`P5.3-protocol-proof-v3.md:236-253`); the minimal repair shape is
  named at `:298` and is "not in this revision, and not authorised by it". Part
  B's FD2(d)/(e) only fail closed for a disconnect **during** a quiesce or an
  empty-at-entry set; a dirty primary that disconnects while an inert secondary
  window remains registered still lets the quiesce settle `ok:true`. The gap
  therefore survives Part B and needs its own decision (F-03).
