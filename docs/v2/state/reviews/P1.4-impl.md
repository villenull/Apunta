## Numbered findings for the implementer

1. **The card's biconditional is not closable, the residual is pinned and
   escalated, and I judge that acceptable — this is a coordinator sentence, not
   implementer work.** The fixed decision reads "In real mode `present` is
   `null` exactly when `reachable` is false", and the deliverer reports that
   the converse cannot hold. That is right, and the reason is sharper than "it
   is hard": the state exists *because of the card's own design*.
   `server/src/ai/ollama.ts:244-260` shows `describe()` reading `/api/tags`
   itself and setting `reachable: false, modelPresent: false` on any failure,
   while `server/src/ai/profiles.ts:62-84` reads the same URL again through its
   own 2 s cache. Two independent reads of one loopback URL is what the card
   mandates (the installed list must be injectable at the route, and
   `model`/`modelPresent` must stay `describe()`-derived), so "reachable, list
   unreadable" is reachable by construction. Had the route been allowed to share
