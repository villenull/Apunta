## IR-03 re-check — FD3's parameter kinds and FD5's blind-spot list

Round 2 filed three separable errors in FD3 and one omission in FD5. Each is now
correct on the facts:

1. **`PrepView.tsx:174` → `dateOnly`, and the card now says why.**
   `PrepView.tsx:174` is `Review due {formatPlanDate(plan.state.data.plan.review_due)}.`,
   and `review_due` is `CalendarDateSchema` (`shared/src/plan.ts:159`, defined at
   `:41-44` as `/^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$/` — a calendar day). `ParamKind` has exactly
   the four values `'text' | 'number' | 'date' | 'dateOnly'`
   (`shared/src/i18n/t.ts:16`); `date` routes to `formatInstant`, which does
   `new Date(text)` and so reads a bare date as UTC midnight, while `dateOnly`
   routes to `formatDay` (`t.ts:245`), which parses the fields and names
   `formatPlanDate` as its oracle — the exact shift west of Greenwich round 2
   described. `dateOnly` is the only kind that keeps `Aug 8, 2026` on the screen
   in every zone, and it is what the card now prescribes.
2. **`BackupCard.tsx:393` → a raw parameter, not a date, and the card now says
   so.** The line is `Restore last tested {instantToLocalDay(lastVerified)}.`
   `instantToLocalDay` (`shared/src/common.ts:33-40`) builds
   `${year}-${month}-${day}` by hand and returns a raw `YYYY-MM-DD`; the
   composed sentence contains **no English date**, so there is nothing to
   reformat. Round 2's point that "each … `date`" was wrong for this line holds,
   and the card now routes it through FD4 as a raw parameter. The screen keeps
   printing `2026-08-01` where it prints it today, and the assertion that
   witnesses it (`BackupCard.test.tsx:145`,
   `toContain('2026-08-01')` on `backup-verified`) stays green; under a `date`
   kind it would not, which is why "alter visible English" is the right
   consequence to name. Note 3 records the one-word tightening available.
