line number is exact**. The card author did correct the coordinator, correctly.

  `BufReader::new(stdout).lines()`, parses each line with the reader's own
  nonce and sends `Event::Bridge(parsed, line)` on the channel, breaking only if
  the send fails. Every line is forwarded, as claimed.

### D1 — the write scope excludes the one change the card's own Fixed decision 6 requires (IR-01, IR-03)

- **Location:** `docs/v2/cards/P3.8.md` "May edit" (`:100-102`), Fixed
  decision 1 (`:164-169`), Fixed decision 6 (`:192-199`), Step S2 (`:243-249`),
  Stop condition 3 (`:314-320`).
- **Conflicting text.** May edit: "confined to the two `break` statements at
  `:298` and `:305` and the `settled` bookkeeping they sit in." S2: "Leave the
  `:305` `break`, leave `settled = true`, leave the `:292` `eprintln!`, leave
  both forwarding arms, leave the `fatal` arm, leave `ChildGone`, the `!settled`
  tail and the terminal drain loop." Fixed decision 6: "a `ChildGone` arriving
  **after** a settled `ready` must still not paint an error screen over a running
  app whose server has just exited … Both halves are asserted in V4. If the
  change cannot tell those two apart, that is Stop condition 3."
- **The conflict, verified in source.** With `:298`'s `break` removed, the `for`
  loop keeps iterating, so the next `ChildGone` the reader thread sends — which
  is what it sends whenever the child exits, `main.rs:250-251` — lands in the
  arm at `:326-336`. That arm is unconditional: `early_exit_code(status)`, then
  `show_error(&handle, &code)` at `:332`, `close_window(&handle, "splash")` at
  `:333`, `settled = true`, `break`. `show_error` builds a real second window
  titled `Apunta — exited_before_ready` (`:614-625`). So the card's own minimal
  change, exactly as S2 specifies it, produces the spurious post-`ready` error
  screen that Fixed decision 6 forbids, V4(e) asserts against, and AM-140 lists
  as owner-mandated ("not painted when it dies after"). The fix needs a
  `settled` test in the `ChildGone` arm (or an equivalent early exit) — which is
  inside `drive` and therefore inside the one May-edit **file**, but outside the
  May-edit **boundary**, and which S2 tells the implementer not to touch.
- **Concrete failure scenario.** A careful implementer follows S2 to the letter,
  deletes one `break`, runs V1 (13/13 green — no Rust test covers `drive`), then
  runs V3, which passes: the marker is forwarded and the ordering holds. They run
  V4, whose (e) fires — a second window titled `Apunta — exited_before_ready`
  appears over the app — so the row reports FAIL and the implementer stops at
  Stop condition 3, having changed nothing wrong and being unable to fix it. The
  card is un-implementable as written on its own central requirement. The
  mirror-image implementer, who adds the guard, has edited outside May edit and
  trips HS-9 and Stop condition 2.
- **Correction.** Amend May edit to read: *"`fn drive` (`:284-361`), and inside
  it only the `ready` arm's control flow and the `ChildGone` arm's `settled`
  test — nothing else."* Amend Fixed decision 1 to state the change as **two**
  parts: (i) remove the success-path `break` at `:298`; (ii) make the
  `ChildGone` arm a no-op when `settled` is already true (log it, do not
  `show_error`, do not `close_window`, and either `continue` or leave the loop
  without painting). Amend S2 to "leave `ChildGone`'s *early-exit* behaviour
  untouched" rather than "leave `ChildGone`", and keep Stop condition 3 as the
  backstop for the case where even that is not expressible. If the coordinator
  decides the card must stay a one-line change, then V4(e), Fixed decision 6's
  second half and AM-140's clause must be dropped together — but that is an owner
  decision (AM-138/AM-140 territory), not one this card or this review may take.

### D2 — the `fatal` arm's post-`ready` behaviour is unspecified (IR-03)

- **Location:** Fixed decision 6 (`:192-199`), Stop condition 3 (`:314-320`),
  V4's Expected cell (`:296`), Read (`:70`, which does name the `fatal` arm).
- **Conflicting or missing text.** Fixed decision 6 says "the **child** and the
  reader thread must stay distinguishable at shutdown" and reasons entirely about
  `ChildGone`. Stop condition 3 says "`ChildGone` arrives on the same channel as
  every line". V4's (d) and (e) are both about the server dying. Nothing anywhere
  says what a `Message::Fatal` arriving **after** a settled `ready` should do —
  and that arm (`:307-317`) is exactly as unconditional as `ChildGone`: `show_error`
  at `:313`, `close_window` at `:314`, `break` at `:316`.
- **Concrete failure scenario.** Removing `:298` makes `fatal` reachable at any
  time after launch. This server's `writeFatal` fires only at startup
  (`server/src/index.ts`'s two sites), so no current code path produces it — but
  a future startup-path failure surfacing late, or a second `ready` misparsed as
  `Fatal`, paints `Apunta — <code>` over a running app and, worse, V4's assertions
  cannot see it because neither (d) nor (e) mentions `fatal`, so a reviewer would
  read the row as green on a shell that can still over-fire rule 7. Silent scope
  gap: the guard is added or not added without the card deciding.
- **Correction.** Add to Fixed decision 6 a third clause, or add V4 assertion
  (f): *a bridge line of any kind arriving after a settled `ready` — `fatal`,
  `ChildGone`, `Other` or a `Rejection` — is logged and ignored; it paints no
  error screen and opens no window.* That formulation covers D1 and D2 together

## Non-defect notes

These are recorded so a later reviewer does not re-raise them, and none of them
is a defect.

1. **The test count in Fixed decision 8 and V1 is right.** `mod tests` is at
   `main.rs:705` and the file holds exactly **13** `#[test]` attributes at this
   base. "13 passed, 0 failed" is the correct expected tally, and because the
   card forbids adding, deleting, renaming, `#[ignore]`ing or loosening any of
   them, **the expected tally is the thing that must be corrected if a future
   run disagrees** — not the tests. (AM-131's "12/12" refers to P3.4's own
   harness, a different file, and is not in conflict.)
2. **Port 7860 is genuinely free** — see the baseline section. Only D6's
   superlative is wrong.
3. **The V3 end-to-end row needs no new code, and it fails on the unmodified
   tree.** `server/src/app.ts:70` with no caller override gives pino's default
   stdout destination, so request URLs go to the child's fd 1 and nowhere else;
   the child's stderr is inherited (`:419`), so a logger line on fd 2 would leak
   past the forwarding loop — it does not. On the base tree the marker therefore
   cannot appear, which makes "**On the unmodified base this row fails**" a true
   control rather than an assertion of intent.
4. **The `ready`-before-marker ordering is a real consumer need, not
   defensiveness.** Fixed decision 4's reasoning is exactly P3.4's: the harness
   needs the boundary between "before ready" and "after ready", and the run-unique
   query string is what makes the marker attributable to the marker request and
   not to the launcher's poll or the page's own fetches.
5. **The `!settled` tail (`:340-348`) and the terminal drain (`:354-360`) are
   correctly left alone**, and the Read section's claim that the drain's
   `Ok(_) => continue` is "the second place the defect shows" is accurate *as a
   description of the base*. After the fix it is simply no longer on the success
   path, which is the point.
6. **Stopping the server after `ready` and stopping the AppImage are different
   experiments.** V4's (d) and (e) both need the *server* stopped while the shell
   lives; the existing harness already distinguishes these (`:578-598`, `:690-701`
   require the shell to exit on its own without a `SIGKILL` escalation), and V4
   should not borrow the shell-stopping idiom by mistake. See D4.
7. **AM-138 and AM-140 agree with each other** on the defect, the ownership, the
   negative-control contents and the port. AM-138's own line reference
   (`main.rs:291-305`) is the coarse one; AM-140 records that the author corrected
   it to `:298` and `:291-306`, which this review confirms against the file.
8. **`build/linux-resources/` and an AppImage are both present**, so V0 and the
   launching rows are runnable now and neither is `NOT RUN` for want of the
   bundle. V0's freshness gate is therefore load-bearing, not decorative: an
   AppImage already sitting in `target/` from an earlier card is exactly the
   stale-binary trap AM-131 records.
9. **The `fatal` and `Other` arms' reachability post-fix** is the substance of
   D2, not a separate concern; a single clause covering "any line after a settled
   `ready`" resolves D1 and D2 together.

## Verdict

**Not fit for implementation dispatch.** **CLEAR 5, DEFECT 5, UNKNOWN 0**
(IR-01 DEFECT, IR-02 CLEAR, IR-03 DEFECT, IR-04 DEFECT, IR-05 DEFECT, IR-06
CLEAR, IR-07 CLEAR, IR-08 CLEAR, IR-09 DEFECT, IR-10 CLEAR).

The card is strong where cards are usually weak: the defect is diagnosed
correctly in source with exact line numbers, the scope is fenced with an
unusually explicit exclusion list, the eight stop conditions anticipate the
specific wrong repairs, `NOT RUN` is never allowed to become `PASS`, the
end-to-end row genuinely needs no new code and genuinely fails on the unmodified
tree, and the thirteen-test tally is correct. The obstacle is narrow and
structural: **the one change the card's own Fixed decision 6 and its
owner-mandated negative control require — making the `ChildGone` arm a no-op
after a settled `ready` — is outside the May-edit boundary and forbidden by
Step S2, so the card as written sends a correct implementer into its own Stop
condition 3 on the first run.** D1 is the blocking defect; D2 is the same
omission one arm over. D3, D4 and D5 are what stop V4 from being executable as
specified; D6 and D7 are accuracy fixes to statements the card makes about its
own verification and its own authorisation; D8 and D9 are what stop attempt 2
from resuming safely. None of these requires widening the card — every
correction stays inside `fn drive`, the two launching rows and the card's own
metadata, and the one genuinely consequential decision (whether the guard counts
as within "the forwarding loop") belongs to the coordinator on AM-138/AM-140,
not to this review.

## Not verified without running something

1. **That `cargo test` currently reports 13 passed, 0 failed.** The count of
   `#[test]` attributes is verified statically; no `cargo` command was run
   because the build lease forbids it.
2. **That `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy -- -D warnings` are clean at this
   base**, and therefore that V1's expected exit 0 is achievable.
3. **That `npm run tauri:build:test` succeeds and produces an AppImage.** An
   AppImage is present on disk from an earlier card, but its provenance and
   freshness against the current sources were not measured — precisely the
   measurement V0 exists to make.
4. **That a request logged by the bundled Fastify writes a URL *including the
   query string* to fd 1.** `logger: true` with pino's default destination is
   verified from `server/src/app.ts:70` and the absence of any caller override;
   the exact log-line shape (whether `req.url` carries `?p38marker=…`) was not
   observed, so V3(b)/(c) are sound in premise but their matcher is untested.
   This is the single most likely surprise for attempt 1.
5. **That the AppImage on disk launches, that a window becomes observable, and
   that `xdotool` is on `PATH` and can read a window's name, geometry and owning
   pid.** `tauri-lifecycle.test.mjs` uses `xdotool` and treats `wmctrl` as a
   fallback focus reader (`:1144-1151`), so its presence is likely, but this
   review started no display and read no window.
6. **That killing the server pid from `main.rs:221` produces `ChildGone` without
   the shell's quit ladder also firing.** The reader thread sends `ChildGone`

   but the interaction with `begin_quit` and the window-close handler at `:504-516`
   was reasoned about, not run.
7. **That the second launch in V4(d) can reliably be caught before `ready`.** This
   is a timing race whose margin was not measured; D4's correction makes the
   assertion about the effect rather than the timing, which should remove the
   dependence, but the margin is unknown.
8. **Whether `xvfb-run` is installed on this host**, and therefore which branch of
   V3's display clause applies. The card requires the row to record which, which
   is the right instruction, but the answer itself is unmeasured — and D3's point
