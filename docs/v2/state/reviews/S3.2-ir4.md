## Defects

### D-11 — FD2b's invented-negation bullet still contains the pre-AM-122 sentence, and it contradicts the corollary, FD8 and V1

- **ID and location.** IR-03. `docs/v2/cards/S3.2.md` line **307** (inside FD2b,
  the `**invented negation**` bullet), i.e. `dispatch/S3.2-ir.md:359`.
- **The conflicting text.** Card `:302-303` says every entry of that control's
  `mustNotContain` "carries the token … **because V1 asserts that every
  `mustNotContain` entry of this control carries the token**". Three lines later,
  card `:307-309`: "an entry whose whole phrase *is* a lexicon term **keeps its
  own authored negator and no token**, which is the only way `jam[aá]s`-shaped
  coverage can exist without a bare negator in `mustNotContain`." The Placement
  corollary at card `:227-231` says the opposite in bold: such a phrase "is
  EXCLUDED from the lexicon-driven invented-negation control's `mustNotContain`
  rather than entered without a token", and adding one "makes **V1 fail**".
- **Failure scenario.** An implementer writes the es-MX
  `positive/02-negacion-inventada.txt` sidecar and, reading the bullet that
  describes exactly that control, adds `"jam[aá]s"` to `mustNotContain` to keep
  the coverage the sentence promises. V1's mandated assertion — every
  `mustNotContain` entry carries the token — fails on the implementer's own
  sidecar. The only HS-7-clean way out is to delete the entry, which is what the
  Placement corollary already mandates, so nothing is broken; but the first
  implementation attempt now spends a cycle on a contradiction between two
  normative sentences of the same card, and if the implementer instead concludes
  the corollary is the wrong one, V3's reserved exit 3 becomes unreachable
  because a token-free entry keeps firing under blinding.
- **Specific correction.** Delete card `:307-309` (the clause beginning "an entry
  whose whole phrase *is* a lexicon term keeps its own authored negator and no
  token") and close the bullet with the corollary's own words: "a phrase with no
  negator slot of its own is excluded from this control, not entered without a
  token; entering one fails V1 by design."
