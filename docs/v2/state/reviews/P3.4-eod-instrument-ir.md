## 2. The instrument identity safety — CLEAR

The candidate replaces the unsatisfiable `getmouselocation … or PID` arm with a
conjunction of three fail-closed facts, all on the **one immutable deadline**:

1. **C1** (`checkReadback`): the **measured native point** `(read.X, read.Y)`
   must be within 1 px of the asked point **and** `containedInWindow(window)`,
   where `window` is the rectangle read once from
   `getwindowgeometry <appWindowId>`. `WINDOW`/`PID` are diagnostic text only.
2. **C2** (`runFlow`): a new deadline-raced `getwindowpid <appWindowId>`
   (classified by `checkCommand`) must return a positive integer **equal to the
   app pid**, verified **before any move is issued**.
3. **C3** (`guardedMove`, unchanged): after the warp, the app's **own page**
   must publish a strictly greater `ptrN` (and, at the target, client coords
   within 2 px).

Independently verified against the actual export (`03`): the real attempt-5
shape (`WINDOW=0`, no `PID`) is accepted **only** when the measured point is
inside the owned rectangle and a fresh page witness arrives; the exact rectangle
edges behave (`x=window.x` in, `x=window.x+w` out); a foreign `WINDOW`/`PID`
inside is diagnostic-only; no X/Y, >1 px, and outside all fail closed. A foreign
owner, a non-integer/zero/negative/empty `getwindowpid`, a non-zero exit, a
signal, and an `XError` each stop the flow with **zero moves and zero clicks**,
and ownership is always read before the first move; a hung `getwindowpid` ends
at the deadline with zero moves. The happy path lands with **exactly one
click**; a stale pointer and a 3 px target witness each mean **zero clicks**.
The shipping baseline, by contrast, never reads the owner pid at all.

**On "can a fake other window trigger the app's own hook?"** No new route is
opened: the `ptrN` facts are read from the **AppImage child's own stderr**, i.e.
the app's bundled server logging the app page's same-origin
`/api/p3.4-observe` requests. A foreign window cannot publish that fact. If a
foreign window overlapped and took the pointer, the app page would not receive
`pointermove`, so C3 fails and no click is issued; if the app is on top, C3 is
satisfied and C2 has already proven the rectangle is the app's. The conjunction
is equal-or-stronger than the removed dead conjunct.
