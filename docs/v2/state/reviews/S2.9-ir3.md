### D1 — The hook pair's mandated hold is the whole serial describe, so `H ≤ 6 s` and the whole V1 arithmetic are false, and the card's only sanctioned response is "report a finding"

- **ID and location.** `docs/v2/state/dispatch/S2.9-ir.md` — §May edit

  **explicitly-requested bullet** (`:312-332`), and §Steps step 2

- **The text that conflicts, or exactly what is missing.** Three places

  never before" (`:309-311`); "an exported `acquire`/`release` pair … that the
  describe calls **around its whole block**" (`:326-327`). Two places say the
  opposite about the same hold: "First, the hold is the row-touching window
  above, **never the whole test body**" (`:276-278`) with "`H` is capped at
  **6 s**" (`:281`), and step 2's "held only for the row-touching window and
  never past it" (`:481-482`). The mandated hold is strictly wider than a test
  body: `brand.spec.ts:293` is `test.describe('rendered colours and 200% zoom')`
  and `:295` is `test.describe.configure({ mode: 'serial' })`, so the hooks and
  all five tests run in **one worker, in order**, and the window between them is
  five tests, not one. Each of the first four does `openHome` (POST
  `/api/formats`, PUT `/api/settings`, `page.goto('/')`, a visibility wait and

### D2 — The five `brand.spec.ts` tests are told to request a lock their own `beforeAll` already holds, and nothing says they may not

- **ID and location.** `docs/v2/state/dispatch/S2.9-ir.md` — §Fixed decisions,
  decision 1's **explicitly-requested bullet** (`:312-332`) against its
  **acquisition** (`:257-260`) and **release** (`:306-311`) bullets and §May
  edit (`:170-177`).
- **The text that conflicts, or exactly what is missing.** The bullet is
  exhaustive by design — "It is requested by **every writer and every asserter of
  that row, in a test or in a hook** … the five in `brand.spec.ts` at `:335`,
  `:343`, `:351`, `:359` and `:398`, **and the describe's own
  `beforeAll`/`afterAll` pair** … Eight tests and one hook pair: that is the
  whole set" (`:314-321`, `:329-330`). But those five tests are inside the group
  whose `beforeAll` the same bullet and §May edit order to acquire the same file,
  and acquisition is `fs.mkdirSync(path)` / `fs.openSync(path, 'wx')` — either it
  creates or it fails with `EEXIST` (`:257-260`) — while "only the holder releases
  it" (`:309-310`) and the release is deferred to the end of `afterAll`
  (`:171-173`). Under `mode: 'serial'` (`:295`) the hooks and those five tests
  run in the **same worker process**, so this is a same-process acquisition of a
  path that process already holds, not merely a cross-worker race. The card
  nowhere says the five are exempt because the hook hold already covers them,
  and nowhere says the helper is re-entrant.
- **One concrete failure scenario.** The implementer follows the enumeration —
  it is a completeness claim and they will rely on it — adds the lock fixture to
  the five brand tests and calls `acquire()` at the top of the group's
  `beforeAll`. Whichever of the two runs first, exactly one waits on a file its
  own process holds: if the hook runs first, every one of the five fixture
  setups polls a lock that will not be released until after the fifth test, so
  all five exhaust the 20 s deadline and fail with the card's own message; if a
  fixture setup runs first, the group's `beforeAll` blocks instead and the whole
  brand spec stalls. V1 and V3 fail on a correct implementation, deterministically
  — 5 tests × 3 repeats in V1, and again in both projects in V3. The reachable
  responses are all closed: dropping the fixture from those five contradicts
  `:314-321` and `:329-330`, adding a per-process re-entrancy guard is not one of
  the six points and reads as widening May edit beyond "a new isolation fixture
  or helper", and a longer deadline is forbidden by `:303-305`.
- **One specific correction or named prerequisite.** One sentence in the same
  bullet, either way — **(a)** "the hook pair's hold covers the five tests inside
  it, so those five do not request the fixture themselves" (then change "Eight
  tests and one hook pair: that is the whole set" so the count is honest: five
  tests request the fixture, the pair calls `acquire`/`release` for its own two
  windows), or **(b)** "the helper is re-entrant: a process that already holds
  the path takes it again without waiting and releases it only when the outermost
  hold ends." (a) is the smaller change and pairs with D1's two-short-holds
  correction, because then nothing overlaps and re-entrancy never arises.
