  between PipeWire step 2 and its teardown, leaving `module-null-sink

  is the re-decision IR-09 exists to prevent.
- **One specific correction:** make the reference resolve. Either (i) restore
  the preflight commands to `nextAllowedAction` when the card is dispatched for
  implementation — they were correct there and cost three sentences — or
  (ii) in `plannedSideEffects[0].resumeInstruction`, replace "see
