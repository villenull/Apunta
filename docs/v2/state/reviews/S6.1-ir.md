## Defects

### D1 — IR-01: the tokeniser the card fixes is not in a May-edit path

- **ID and location:** `docs/v2/cards/S6.1.md` (and its twin
  `docs/v2/state/dispatch/S6.1-ir.md`) → "May edit" and "Fixed decisions",
  bullet 3.
- **The text that conflicts, or exactly what is missing:** May edit grants
  `web/src/lib/speller.ts` "and test", `web/package.json`,
  `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`, `scripts/collect-licenses.mjs` and
  `docs/decisions.md`. The Fixed decision "Tokeniser keeps á é í ó ú ü ñ and
  treats ¿ ¡ as punctuation; Unicode normalisation to NFC" is a property of a
  tokeniser that is **not** in `speller.ts`:
  `web/src/lib/spelling.ts` holds it —
  `const WORD = /[A-Za-z]+(?:['’][A-Za-z]+)*/g;` plus `plainWord()` and
  `allowedWords()`, which re-tokenises every allow-list entry with the same
  ASCII-only regex. `web/src/lib/spelling.ts` and `web/src/lib/spelling.test.ts`
  are outside May edit.
- **One concrete failure scenario:** the worker implements Spanish entirely
  inside `speller.ts`, as May edit permits. V1 then passes (`nspell` is handed
  a whole word by the test), but in the app a Spanish note is tokenised by the
  ASCII regex, so `sesión` becomes the two tokens `sesi` + `n`, both flagged,
  `¿Cómo` yields `C` + `mo`, and the patient's allow-listed name `José Ramírez`
  contributes `jos` + `ramirez` while the typed text `José` is never looked up.
  Every Spanish word with an accent is broken, in all four fields, and no
  May-edit file can fix it.
- **One specific correction or named prerequisite:** add
  `web/src/lib/spelling.ts` and `web/src/lib/spelling.test.ts` to May edit,
  scoped to the `WORD`/`plainWord`/`allowedWords` tokeniser and its tests only;
  and state where NFC normalisation happens (a `String.normalize('NFC')` in
  `plainWord`, or in `speller.ts` before `nspell`), because "without removing
  accents" forbids folding and must be checkable.
