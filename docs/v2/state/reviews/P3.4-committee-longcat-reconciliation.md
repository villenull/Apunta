## The calibration disagreement — resolved in Space Bunny's favour

The coordinator asked me to test my one-point offset conceptually. It fails:

- Model client→native per axis as `S = k·c + o`. Two unknowns.
- One warp gives one equation per axis. Requiring the page's `screenX ≈ asked` is satisfied at the probe point **for any `k`** — it constrains nothing. So the one-point method cannot separate scale from offset, and its prediction at the target errs by `(k−1)·(c_probe − c_target)`. With `k ≠ 1` it issues a **wrong click**, not a NOT RUN. That is the exact defect class (a check that cannot fail) this card keeps paying for.
- Two warps at distinct client coordinates recover `k` and `o` exactly per axis; `0 < k ≤ 4` plus the ≤1 px read-back make it fail-closed. The extra warp costs ~1 poll and is irrelevant under one 30 s deadline.
- **Therefore: adopt two-point affine; withdraw one-point.** Bootstrap warps press nothing; both are containment-checked before warping (Space Bunny's §2 S6 intent — its §3.2 table should say so for `BOOTSTRAP_1` too, an internal wording fix, not a design dispute).
