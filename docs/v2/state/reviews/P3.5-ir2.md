- **The gate itself is correct and its evidence holds.** No producer of a bundle
  anywhere in this repository sets `VITE_APUNTA_TEST_IDENTITY`; Vite 8.2.2
  replaces an absent `VITE_`-prefixed name with the literal `undefined` at build

  (`server/src/http/request-guard.ts:32`), exactly as the card says. D1 and D9 are
  about **using** and **not restoring** the gate, not about the gate.
- **Reading capture state out of the DOM is strong enough for the positive arm.**
  The card reads what the app already renders, and each datum is a projection of
  the hook's own state rather than a re-derivation: the phase is
  `useLiveRecording`'s `phase`, `record-timer` is `setSeconds(elapsed)` from the
  recorder's `onProgress`, and `--level` is `setLevel(presence)`. A timer that
  advances is itself proof that frames arrived, and `--level > 0.01` is proof that
  the frames were non-zero — so the DOM is the right place to read, and no React
  state is being bypassed. The gap is not the source of truth (D5b) and not *which
  device* (D3).
