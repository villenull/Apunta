## DEFECTs

### D-15 — FD2b states the placement rule twice, and the two statements have different scope

- **ID and location.** `docs/v2/cards/S3.2.md:313-316`, the **invented negation**
  bullet under FD2b's "The lexicon supplies vocabulary only" pair, read against
  the Placement corollary at `:227-229` and the lost-negation bullet at
  `:317-323`.
- **Conflicting text.** The corollary is properly qualified: a phrase with no
  negator slot "is EXCLUDED from **the lexicon-driven invented-negation
  control's `mustNotContain`**". The bullet restates it as "excluded from **a
  lexicon-keyed control**", unqualified, and adds "There is **no second
  allowance** here". Both es-MX negation sidecars are `lexicon`-keyed
  (`:266-270`, `:803`), and the lost-negation control's own worked JSON
  (`:373`) puts two token-less entries in `mustNotContain` — one of them,
  `"camina toda la noche"`, with no negator at all — which the lost-negation
  bullet then explicitly authorises (`:319-320`, "sidecar-authored, **no**
  token"). So the literal scope of `:314` forbids an entry the card's own example
  contains, and "no second allowance" reads as a universal.
- **One concrete failure scenario.** An implementer reads `:314` as the whole rule
  (it says the corollary "is the whole of it") and, finding
  `"camina toda la noche"` to be a phrase with no negator slot on a
  `lexicon`-keyed sidecar, deletes it from the lost-negation control. The control
  then detects nothing through its `mustNotContain` half; V2's
  "7 positive `flagged`" expectation and FD8's "the **lost-negation** control is
  **expected to stay flagged**" both become unreachable, and FD6's admissibility
  rule turns the run into exit 3 for a reason that has nothing to do with the
  scorer.
- **One specific correction.** At `:313-314`, replace "excluded from a
  lexicon-keyed control" with "excluded from **that control's `mustNotContain`**"
  — matching `:228-229` word for word — and delete or scope "There is no second
  allowance here" to the invented-negation control's list, so the two statements
  are the same statement.

### D-16 — the controls loader is a third `scoreNote` call site, and nothing requires the locale terms there

- **ID and location.** `docs/v2/cards/S3.2.md`, FD6 "The sidecar type is narrower"
  (`:707-724`) and the sidecar-key table (`:746-755`), read against FD2c's
  locale-scoped loader (`:411-425`) and `score.ts:119-121`.
- **What is missing.** D-13 fixed the pipeline runner's call. The card now
  requires the locale-scoped terms at two of the three places notes are scored:
  `run.ts:144` (provider, `:58-65`, `:107-109`) and FD4 step 4 (pipeline,
  `:583-588`). The third is the controls loader, and FD6 says only that
  "`scoreNote(fixture: Fixture, …)` … [is] called **unchanged** … with **no
  parameter added and no branch inside either function**" — a sentence an
  implementer reads as *pass nothing extra*. Compounding it, FD2c makes

  calls it with no argument at `score.ts:120`; the card never says what that
  no-argument call resolves to once the loader is locale-scoped, nor that the
  controls' options must carry the run's terms.
- **One concrete failure scenario.** The controls loader calls
  `scoreNote(fixture, sections)` as FD6 reads. Under `--locale es-MX` the absent
  `lexicon` option falls through to `loadLexicon()`, which yields the **English**

  gate at all**" is false in the implementation that ships.
- **One specific correction.** Add one sentence to FD6's "The sidecar type is

  A V1 assertion that an es-MX control's score differs from the same control
