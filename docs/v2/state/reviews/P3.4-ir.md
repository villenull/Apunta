- **ID and location:** `cards/P3.4.md:35` (V1 expected "CSP on app HTML and

  submitted first. Either way the row's expected result is not decidable from
  the instructions.
- **Correction:** name the decision in the card — either a per-response nonce or
  hash for the boot page's `<style>`, or `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` justified
