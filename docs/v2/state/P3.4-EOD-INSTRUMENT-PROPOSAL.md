### 2.1 Every actual change, named

| # | Region (shipping line) | Change | Why |

| C1 | `checkReadback` `:1029-1054` | Drop the dead `or PID` conjunct. Require the **measured native point** (`read.X/read.Y`) to be `containedInWindow(window)`. Keep the ≤1 px X/Y check. `WINDOW`/`PID` become **diagnostic text only** (never a pass condition). | The rectangle is read from `getwindowgeometry <appWindowId>` and its owner is verified in C2; the page witness (C3) is the live "over the app" fact. |

**Unchanged, deliberately:** the immutable 30 s deadline; exactly-one-click; the

IPC assertion (already PASS, AM-188); the five-row `NOT RUN` cascade; every

assertions; the navigation and window-open rows. No test is deleted or weakened.

### 2.2 Why this is not a loosening (HS-7)

Identity before the click is the conjunction of **three** facts, all
fail-closed:

1. **C2**: the measured rectangle belongs to the app pid — a native
   `getwindowpid` re-read of the exact window id, not a page claim.
2. **C1**: the pointer was actually measured at a point **inside** that
   rectangle, within 1 px of the asked point.
3. **C3**: the app's **own hook** published a fresh `ptrN` (and, at the target,
   client coords within 2 px) **after** the warp.

A genuine wrong-window click is refused: a point outside the rectangle fails C1,
and an **overlapping foreign window cannot satisfy C3** — the app's own page
never receives the pointermove, so the move ends at the deadline with no click.
The one thing removed is a conjunct that **could never be true for a real run**;
the one thing added is a real native owner check anchoring the measured
rectangle (C2), which is stronger than trusting the cursor's own `WINDOW` field
that this host cannot resolve. Net: equal-or-stronger, and finally satisfiable.

## 6. Bounded risk and unknowns (stated, not discovered later)

- The synthetic proof shows the **logic** accepts the real measured shape and
  refuses wrong ones. It does **not** prove that a warp-driven `pointermove`
  reaches the WebKitGTK page under `xvfb-run` with no window manager, that the
  target click lands, or the real `getwindowpid` value — those are exactly what
  attempt 6's V2 measures, and their being false is a named `FAIL`/`NOT RUN`
  with no click, never a mis-click (final diagnosis §6, proposal-v5 §K).
- The exact reason `getmouselocation` returns `WINDOW=0` here remains
  medium-confidence/unproven; the correction **does not depend on knowing it**,
  because identity no longer rests on that field.
- This correction is the **minimal existing route**. If a reviewer cannot prove
  it safe, the answer is **park (Option B)** — not a new calibration scheme, a
  new module or a rewritten flow.
- **One residual, recorded not hidden:** `D7` is still *named* `'D7 native
  read-back needs 1 px, the app window or the app pid'`, and under the corrected
  instrument the "or the app pid" arm is gone (`PID` is diagnostic text only).
  The repair preserved all 80 test names as instructed and re-derived only that
  test's assertions. Renaming the single string would change no assertion and no
  count, and is available as a one-line follow-up if the owner prefers it.
