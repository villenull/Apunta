## Why the row exists, restated for this attempt

`t()` falls back to **English** outside a test build by design, so a
half-finished catalogue puts English on screen and every other row stays green.
That is precisely the failure attempt 2 shipped with four rows passing: four
English sentences on a Spanish install, one of them the first line of every
successful refine reply. V4 cannot see a missing sentence, only an unfinished
catalogue — which is why the proof that none is left is mechanical extraction
plus runtime rendering, in `attempt-3-no-english-left.md`, and not this row.
