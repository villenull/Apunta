the case reads both sides. With `settings.language` = `es-MX` and a route that
fails with `code: 'ENOSPC'`:

```
reply  507 storage_error
       Apunta no puede escribir en <sandbox>/apunta-errors-test-XXXX porque el
       disco está lleno. Libera espacio e inténtalo de nuevo. Tus datos
       existentes quedaron intactos.

log    {"level":50,...,"code":"ENOSPC","msg":"Apunta cannot write to
       <sandbox>/apunta-errors-test-XXXX because the disk is full. Free space
       and try again. Your existing data was left untouched."}
```

The reply is the request's language; the pino line is the English catalogue's,
character for character, whatever the request's language was. The case also
asserts the Spanish reply and the English log differ, so it cannot pass by both
being English. Before the fix the same case is red on
`not.toContain('el disco está lleno')` — the log carried the Spanish sentence.
