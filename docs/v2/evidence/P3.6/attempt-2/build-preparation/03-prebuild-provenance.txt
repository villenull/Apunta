Purpose: the card forbids borrowing an earlier test image, and Stop 3 forbids
launching an artefact left behind by another card's run. The identity guard at
the end of V0 cannot distinguish those two cases, because the artefact NAME is
derived from the config and a leftover and a fresh build share it. Hash, size and
mtime are therefore what make the distinction, and they had to exist before the
build to mean anything.
