## 5. What fixture.patch actually does (three edits, one file, +15/-8)

(a) A1 — flowOps() now supplies the new MANDATORY ownership seam. runFlow calls
    ops.windowPid(window.id) unconditionally, so the seam must exist:

      windowPid: () => ({ code: 0, signal: null, stdout: `${String(appPid)}\n`, stderr: '' }),

    placed directly after the windowGeometry entry. Nothing else about the fake
    changes: same shape, same signal field, no hang, no error path. (The
    fail-closed behaviour of a foreign / non-integer / non-zero / XError
    getwindowpid is proven by the review's 32 adversarial controls against the
    real export, not by this happy-path fake.)

(b) A2 — D7's two identity assertions are re-derived for the corrected
    semantics, and the containment NEGATIVE that replaces their strength is
    added. Under the candidate, the getmouselocation WINDOW/PID fields are
    diagnostic text only, so "PID: -1 rejects" and "WINDOW: 999 rejects" are no
    longer true statements about the instrument. In their place:

      assert.equal(... PID: '-1'  ..., ok, true);      // foreign pid is diagnostic
      assert.equal(... WINDOW:'999'..., ok, true);      // foreign window is diagnostic
      assert.match(checkReadback({x:50,y:500}, {X:'50',Y:'500',WINDOW:'999'}, ...).reason,
                   /outside the owned app window/);    // leaving the owned rectangle fails closed

    The new negative is strictly load-bearing: x=50 is outside WINDOW.x=60 while
    the 1 px read-back is exact, so the containment clause is what refuses it.
    D7's other four assertions (1 px in, 2 px out, app-pid named, no usable X/Y)
    are untouched, and D7's test NAME is preserved verbatim — see the noted
    residual in 06.

(c) A3 — six `dispatched` deep-equal arrays now include the new command, in
    dispatch order, immediately after getwindowgeometry (line numbers in the
    REPAIRED file): F2 (:1135), F2-d (:1175), F3 refusedFirst (:1255), F3
    refusedSecond (:1262), F6 (:1318), F7 (:1330). Each still asserts the FULL
    array, so the new dispatch is pinned in both directions; the two arrays that
    must NOT grow — :1125 (the F1-g getwindowgeometry classification case) and
    :1382 — are left exactly as they are, which is itself evidence the
    correction is scoped.
