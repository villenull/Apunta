## Research rules (all S1 cards)
- Cite every factual claim with its URL. Prefer primary sources (Diario
  Oficial de la Federación, gob.mx, Secretaría de Salud, professional bodies,
  university faculties, official project pages).
- Label each finding `[verified]`, `[inferred]` (say from what) or
  `[not found]` (say what was checked). Never fill a gap with a guess:
  write "Data not available, flag for the owner".
- Public pages only; no logins; no downloads (HS-3).
- No real clinical records or text that looks like one. Describe structure.
- Competitor sites (Kalyo, MentalGest, Luna) for terminology and tone only;
  never copy their text.
- No legal advice: report what a rule says and whom it covers; list open
  questions for a Mexican lawyer.
