## Fixed decisions
- Written from scratch in natural spoken Mexican Spanish (fillers, restarts,
  out-of-order narration), not translated line by line from English.
- Organise fixtures by trap type: `<split>/<trap-type>/<name>.txt`. Author
  **at least 5 fixtures per trap type** before splitting. Within each trap
  type, sort by name and hold out indices `i` where `i % 5` is 1 or 3
  (exactly 40%); the rest are tuning. Record the resulting lists.
- Trap types (C-EVAL rule 4 and more): lost negation, invented negation,
  dose and number (decimals and units), experiencer (patient vs a family
  member), past vs current risk, uncertainty, spoken correction (S1.3
  markers), a section never covered, an English loanword, unclear speech,
  and clean controls. The English corpora's total fixture count is the
  minimum total.
- NAMES.md lists every fictional name used; nothing else appears.
