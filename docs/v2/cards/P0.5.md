## Objective
Make the whole suite pass under every zone by fixing the four test files whose
fixed-UTC-instant expectations assume a zone west of Greenwich. The card is the
follow-up P0.2's review recommended: P0.2's own change is correct; the six
Sydney failures live in files outside its May-edit list.

proves a real day-boundary bug — then stop and report. Anything else.

## Fixed decisions
- Apply P0.2's pattern: each affected `describe` sets and restores
  `process.env.TZ` in `beforeEach`/`afterEach`. **No global `TZ` pin.**
- Keep every assertion asserting the same local-day contract; do not weaken a
  test to make it pass. Choose the minimal change (pin the zone, or make the
  expectation zone-correct) that preserves the assertion's meaning.
- The four files are test code only; production code must not change (the six
  failures were verified as test-code causes in P0.2).
