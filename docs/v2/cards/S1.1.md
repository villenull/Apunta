records, which rules apply, and Spanish section names for the owner's seven
sections, SOAP and intake.

## Questions
1. Terms for a session note and the record (nota de evolución, nota de
   sesión, expediente clínico, historia clínica); "paciente", "consultante"
   or "usuario" in private practice.
2. NOM-004-SSA3-2012: scope, whether it covers psychologists outside health
   establishments, required progress-note content, retention period. The
   Sociedad Mexicana de Psicología ethics code on records. The 2025 data
   protection law only as context.
3. Common progress-note structures (SOAP in Spanish, narrative) and their
   usual section names.
4. Proposed Spanish names for: Location, Client presentation, Risk review,
   Discussion, Intervention, Out of session actions, Note for next session
   (the seven in `shared/src/note-format.ts`); and for the SOAP sections
   (`Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan` per `BY_SECTIONS` in
   `server/src/ai/default-instructions.ts`) and the intake sections
   (`Presenting problem, History, Formulation, Plan` per `server/src/seed.ts`
   line 44). One table with source or reasoning per name.

## Research rules (all S1 cards)
- Cite every factual claim with its URL. Prefer primary sources (Diario
  Oficial de la Federación, gob.mx, Secretaría de Salud, professional bodies,
  university faculties, official project pages).
- Label each finding `[verified]`, `[inferred]` (say from what) or
  `[not found]` (say what was checked). Never fill a gap with a guess:
  write "Data not available, flag for the owner".
- Public pages only; no logins; no downloads (HS-3).
- No real clinical records or text that looks like one. Describe structure.
- Competitor sites (Kalyo, MentalGest, Luna) for terminology and tone only;
  never copy their text.
- No legal advice: report what a rule says and whom it covers; list open
  questions for a Mexican lawyer.
