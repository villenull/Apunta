### Added by AM-051 (owner-approved, 2026-09-27) — the oracle, not just the strings

Two `es-MX` values from S2.4 render "de 1 notas" for a single note, and one
plural form omits `{total}` while its siblings carry it and `kind` declares it.
The defect is small; the *mechanism* is the point, and it is the identical blind
spot that hid S2.5's three-part English across three attempts: **`t.test.ts:57-66`
unions `{name}` tokens across `text` and every plural form into one Set and
compares the union**, so a mismatch confined to one category cannot fail. An
oracle weaker than the property it checks.

So this card does both halves, in this order:

1. **Fix the oracle first.** Compare per form — each of `text` and every plural
   category — rather than the union. A test that gets stricter can then be
   relied on to mean something.
2. **Then correct the two `es-MX` values** so each form's tokens match its
   `kind`.

**The order is the requirement, and the reason is checkable.** If the strings
are fixed first, the still-unioning oracle reports green and proves nothing,
and the latent hole ships inside the same commit that was supposed to close it.
Fixing the oracle first means the string fix is *verified* by a test that can
actually fail. If fixing the oracle surfaces further per-form mismatches beyond
the two known, fix them in this card and say so in the return — do not widen the
oracle back to a union to make them pass.

### Added by AM-053 (owner decision, 2026-09-27) — the accent picker's contrast warning

**Scheduled, not built.** The key `settings.accentLowContrast` is already in
both catalogues (landed in `199e6c6`); nothing renders it. This card builds the
behaviour, because `Settings.tsx` is in its May-edit list and nowhere else.

- The warning appears under the colour picker when the chosen accent is under
  **3:1 against the page surface in either theme** — against the real tokens
  `#faf9f5` (light) and `#151515`/`#111111` (dark), **not** against white or
  black, which is what a naive implementation would reach for and would be
  wrong.
- **Warn, not refuse.** The colour can still be kept. This is the owner's
  explicit choice: a hard refusal on a colour she picked is worse than a note.
- It names the logo, because `--brand-mark` now resolves to `var(--accent)` (D10
  as amended 2026-09-27), so a colour hard to see on the page takes the wordmark
  with it. Say so in the copy rather than leaving the connection implicit.
- The sentence is already decided and already translated. **Do not invent
  another string, and do not re-add the key.**
- No threshold in `CONTRACTS.md` moves and no acceptance row above changes.

**A note on scope.** Neither amendment is a preference; both are defects
reached from a review. This card is the first one that can write the paths
where they live, which is why it can write them at all. If that is not true of
the paths at implementation time, report `BLOCKED` and name the plan-editor
action rather than editing around the card.
