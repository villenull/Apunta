synthetic English speech, and silence or a tone never produces a note.

## Fixed decisions
- Fixture: Piper (A09) with the `en_US` voice (A10), text: a fabricated
  30-second progress dictation about John Smith with one negated risk
  statement and one dose. If the voice's model card does not permit
  committing generated audio, generate it at test time into the sandbox.
- Feed audio through a virtual PulseAudio or PipeWire source created by the
  test and removed afterwards.
- Grant microphone access only when the requesting origin is the app's own
  `http://127.0.0.1:<port>`.
- GStreamer plugins (A03) only if a failing test shows they are missing; the
  `apt` command goes to OWNER-ACTIONS.
