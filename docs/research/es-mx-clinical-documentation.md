## 2. Q1 — What things are called

### 2.1 The session note: *nota de evolución*

The binding term for a session note is **nota de evolución** `[verified]` — NOM-004 §6.2: *"Deberá elaborarla el médico cada vez que proporciona atención al paciente ambulatorio"* followed by the six required elements (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787). The UNAM FES Iztacala SOAP teaching material describes SOAP notes as *"Consideradas también como Notas de evolución"* and *"Fácilmente adaptable al trabajo del psicólogo"* `[verified]` (https://www.medicinaconductual-unam-fesi.org/uploads/1/0/3/4/103420148/elaboraci%C3%B3n_de_nota_soap_y_examen_mental.pdf). The federal CONASAMA guide standardises a ***Nota de evolución psicológica*** as a named annex (Anexo 19) `[verified]` (https://www.conasama.salud.gob.mx/Residenciales/Guxa_expediente_res_profesional_13_09_21.pdf).

*Nota de sesión* is the colloquial/private-practice synonym (used across vendor and clinic sites); it appears nowhere in the norm's numerals `[inferred from absence across the DOF text extracts — weak evidence, flagged, not a finding]`. Practical consequence: a Spanish format named *"Nota de evolución"* is the legally legible title; *"Nota de sesión"* is friendlier but has no normative hook. Flag for the owner which she prefers.

### 2.2 The record: *expediente clínico*, opened by a *historia clínica*

- **Expediente clínico** is the record as a whole `[verified]` — NOM-004 §4.4: *"al conjunto único de información y datos personales de un paciente, que se integra dentro de todo tipo de establecimiento para la atención médica, ya sea público, social o privado…"* (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787).
- **Historia clínica** is the opening document of that record `[verified]` — NOM-004 §6.1 (contenido de la historia clínica: ficha, antecedentes, padecimiento actual, exploración, diagnósticos, pronóstico, indicación terapéutica), same URL.
- **Expediente psicológico** is the profession's own term for the psychology file `[verified]` — FENAPSIME ethics code art. 15 requires elaborating and safeguarding *"un expediente psicológico del usuario"* (https://fenapsime.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Codigo-de-Etica-FENAPSIME.pdf).
- **Historia clínica psicológica** is the standard vendor/practice name for the psychology intake document (Kalyo, hclin.info); it is a descriptive label, not a term the norm defines `[inferred from vendor usage; the norm only says historia clínica]`.

### 2.3 *Paciente*, *usuario*, *consultante*: who is who

- The norm uses **paciente** everywhere and defines it **in terms of usuario**: §4.7 *"Paciente, a todo aquel usuario beneficiario directo de la atención médica"*; §4.12 *"Usuario, a toda aquella persona, que requiera y obtenga la prestación de servicios de atención médica"* `[verified]` (https://www.medigraphic.com/pdfs/conamed/con-2022/con223g.pdf). In NOM-004 the two words are near-synonyms, with *paciente* the working term.
- The profession's ethics codes say **usuario**: the SMP code frames itself as protection of *"los usuarios de los servicios psicológicos"* `[verified]` (https://sociedadmexicanadepsicologia.org/index.php/publicaciones/codigo-etico), and the FENAPSIME code uses *usuario* consistently (arts. 3–11, 15) `[verified]` (https://fenapsime.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Codigo-de-Etica-FENAPSIME.pdf).
- **Consultante** belongs to the humanistic/person-centred tradition: Rogers dropped *paciente* (passive, sick-role) for *cliente*/*consultante*, a usage carried on in Mexican humanistic training centres `[verified]` (https://adipa.mx/noticias/psicologia-humanista-que-es/ ; https://revistadepsicologiagepu.es.tl/Psicoterapia-y-Acompa%F1amiento-d--Un-An%E1lisis-Conceptual-desde-el-Humanismo-y-la-Teor%EDa-de-la-Autodeterminaci%F3n.htm). It signals a non-pathologising stance, not a legal category.
- Patient-facing private-practice marketing overwhelmingly says **paciente** (Doctoralia's terms mediate between *"los pacientes y los Psicólogos"* `[verified]` https://terapia.doctoralia.com.mx/terminos-y-condiciones; Psicologic México addresses *"cada paciente"* `[verified]` https://psicologicmx.com/servicios-de-terapia-psicologica).

**Bottom line for the app** `[inferred]`: default to *paciente* in any Spanish UI or format (it matches the norm, the owner's English "Patient", and private-practice convention); keep *consultante* available only if the owner explicitly wants the humanistic framing. This is a one-question flag for her, not a research gap. Note the consequence: the owner's English formats say "Client" (her voice document) while her SOAP/intake formats say "Patient" — the Spanish default should follow whichever English word the corresponding format uses, i.e. *consultante*-family for her own format is wrong; *paciente* matches SOAP/intake and *cliente* would match her own format most literally. §5.4 records the recommendation per section.

## 3. Q2 — Which rules apply

### 3.1 NOM-004-SSA3-2012: scope

- Published in the DOF **15 October 2012**, in force since 2013 `[verified]` (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787).
- Scope, verbatim (§2, *Campo de aplicación*): *"Esta norma, es de observancia obligatoria para el personal del área de la salud y los establecimientos prestadores de servicios de atención médica de los sectores público, social y privado, incluidos los consultorios."* `[verified]` (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787). No size floor: a one-room consultorio is named.
- **It names psychology explicitly, twice.** §5.3 obliges *"El médico, así como otros profesionales o personal técnico que intervengan en la atención del paciente"* `[verified]` (https://cri.saludtlax.gob.mx/images/NORMATIVA_20/NORMA_EXPEDIENTE), and the §6.1 exploration numeral requires *"específicamente la información que corresponda a la materia del odontólogo, psicólogo, nutriólogo y otros profesionales de la salud"* `[verified]` (same URL).
- **The psychology-specific flexibility clause is §5.16**, verbatim: *"Para el caso de los expedientes de atención psicológica, de nutriología o similares, que se integren en un establecimiento para la atención médica ambulatoria independiente o no ligado a un establecimiento hospitalario, tanto la historia clínica como las notas de evolución, se ajustarán a la naturaleza de los servicios prestados, atendiendo a los principios científicos y éticos que orientan la práctica médica."* `[verified]` (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787).
- Psychology is a listed health profession in **LGS art. 79** (*"…química, psicología, optometría…"*) `[verified]` (https://www.dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle.php?codigo=5584753&fecha=24/01/2020).

**What this changes vs the sibling file** `[inferred]`: the old file's §1 framed applicability as "very probably binds her, but genuinely debated in commentary." The text itself is stronger than that framing suggests — §5.16 contemplates *exactly her situation* (independent ambulatory psychological care) and regulates it rather than exempting it. The honest residual doubt is narrower: whether a solo *licenciada en psicología* who never filed a COFEPRIS *aviso de funcionamiento* counts as an *establecimiento* or as *personal del área de la salud* (the §2 subject that needs no establishment at all). That is lawyer question §7.1, but the working assumption "assume NOM-004 applies" is now grounded in §5.16, not in commentary.

### 3.2 Required progress-note content

Every note must carry (all `[verified]` from the DOF text, https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787 unless noted):

- **Identification of the patient on every note/report** (§5.9): nombre completo, edad, sexo, and bed/file number where applicable.
- **Date, time, full name of the author, and signature** (§5.10): *"Todas las notas en el expediente clínico deberán contener fecha, hora y nombre completo de quien la elabora, así como la firma autógrafa, electrónica o digital, según sea el caso."* Note: **no cédula** in this numeral — see §3.4.
- **Form** (§5.11): *"lenguaje técnico-médico, sin abreviaturas, con letra legible, sin enmendaduras ni tachaduras y conservarse en buen estado."*
- **Electronic records are explicitly optional** (§5.12): *"De manera optativa, se podrán utilizar medios electrónicos, magnéticos, electromagnéticos, ópticos, magneto-ópticos o de cualquier otra tecnología…"*
- **Providers may design their own formats** (§5.13): *"podrán elaborar formatos para el expediente clínico, tomando en cuenta los requisitos mínimos establecidos en esta norma."* This is the normative hook for Apunta's custom formats.
- **The progress-note content** (§6.2, *Nota de evolución*): 6.2.1 evolución y actualización del cuadro clínico; 6.2.2 signos vitales según se considere necesario; 6.2.3 resultados relevantes de estudios auxiliares solicitados previamente; 6.2.4 diagnósticos o problemas clínicos; 6.2.5 pronóstico; 6.2.6 tratamiento e indicaciones médicas (medicamentos: dosis, vía, periodicidad). Caveat, new since the sibling file: the numeral says *"Deberá elaborarla el médico…"* — for a psychology file §5.16 is what adapts this to her practice, which is why e.g. vital signs are *"según se considere necesario"* and effectively absent.
- **Resumen clínico** (§4.10 + §5.6): a doctor-authored document with *padecimiento actual, diagnósticos, tratamientos, evolución, pronóstico, estudios de laboratorio y gabinete*, provided on **written request** (*"Cuando se requiera un resumen clínico u otras constancias del expediente clínico, deberá ser solicitado por escrito."*).

### 3.3 Retention: five years from the last act

§5.4, verbatim: *"…deberán ser conservados por un periodo mínimo de 5 años, contados a partir de la fecha del último acto médico."* `[verified]` (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787). Ownership gloss in the same numeral: records belong to the institution or to *"el prestador de servicios médicos que los genera, cuando éste, no dependa de una institución"* — i.e. a solo practitioner's files are hers, with the patient holding titularity rights over the information.

### 3.4 The cédula question: resolved as far as the text allows

- The binding note-content numeral (§5.10) requires **full name and signature only — no cédula** `[verified]` (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787).
- The cédula **does** appear in the norm, but only in **Appendix A (Informativo), the non-binding quality-evaluation checklist**: item D2-11 (historia clínica) and item D12-17 (nota de egreso) list *"Nombre completo, cédula profesional y firma del médico"* `[verified]` (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5272787). §5.20 introduces that appendix as a voluntary quality-evaluation tool (*"se podrá evaluar la calidad…"*), so checklist items are not obligations.
- Federal practice, however, prints it: the Secretaría de Salud CONASAMA guide's note formats require *"Nombre, firma y número de cédula profesional del profesional de salud"* and its notes chapter summarises note elements including *"nombre, firma y cédula profesional"* `[verified]` (https://www.conasama.salud.gob.mx/Residenciales/Guxa_expediente_res_profesional_13_09_21.pdf). That guide governs addiction residential establishments, not consultorios — practice evidence, not a rule for her.
- So the sibling file's §3.2 ("unresolved") resolves to: **the norm's binding text never requires the cédula on a progress note; federal health-sector practice includes it routinely** `[inferred from the above]`. Practical recommendation unchanged: keep a *Cédula profesional* field and print it on anything that could enter the expediente (it is already required on her wall by LGS art. 79 ¶2 and on every CFDI she issues), but never claim NOM-004 requires it per note. Lawyer question §7.3 stays open at low priority.

### 3.5 The ethics codes: the profession's own record-keeping rules

SMP *Código Ético del Psicólogo* (the most-cited code in Mexico `[verified]` — https://sociedadmexicanadepsicologia.org/index.php/nosotros/conaep):

- Art. 53: document professional/scientific work appropriately to allow later service provision, accountability, and legal/institutional compliance `[verified]` (https://ciberpsicologia.unam.mx/SimuladoresLevyc/etico.pdf).
- Art. 54: create, maintain, disseminate, store, retain and dispose of files/records *in accordance with the law* and the code `[verified]` (same URL).
- Confidentiality chapter: due confidentiality when creating, storing, retrieving, transferring and deleting records, including protecting electronic records against intrusion `[verified]` (same URL).
- Scope: binding on members adjudicated through CONAEP (which *"no tiene facultades judiciales"* — sanctions are ethical, against members only) `[verified]` (https://sociedadmexicanadepsicologia.org/index.php/nosotros/conaep). Whether the owner is an SMP member is `[not found]` — flag for her; if she is not a member, the SMP code binds her only as professional best practice, not jurisdictionally.

FENAPSIME *Código de ética* (federation-level code, directly psychology-specific) `[verified]` (https://fenapsime.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Codigo-de-Etica-FENAPSIME.pdf):

- Art. 7: inform the user of place, schedule, duration, cost and methods; **extending an agreement or modifying the *plan de trabajo inicial* requires advance discussion and the user's consent**.
- Art. 8: back the planning with printed evidence; document **session duration, period, and care/treatment model**; written contracts/agreements in the file.
- Art. 10: present a **plan de trabajo** suited to the user's needs, specifying times, stages, methods, techniques and strategies.
- Art. 15: keep a norm-compliant **expediente psicológico** (service type, testimonial evidence, psychometry…).
- Art. 29: intervention-plan elements (diagnosis, history, information gathering, resources, strategy outline, stages, timeline).

`[inferred]` This confirms and sharpens the sibling file's §5.3: the *plan de trabajo* is a documented, consented, filed artefact with named elements — the Mexican home of anything plan-shaped, and it is client-facing.

### 3.6 The 2025 data-protection law, as context only

- The 2010 LFPDPPP was **abrogated**; the new *Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares* was published in the DOF **20 March 2025**, in force the next day `[verified]` (https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf ; https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/abro/lfpdppp_2010/LFPDPPP_abro_20mar25.pdf).
- Health data is sensitive by statutory definition: *"se consideran sensibles los datos personales que puedan revelar… estado de salud presente o futuro…"* `[verified]` (https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf).
- Authority: INAI is gone; functions pass to the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno** (federal guarantor authority *"Transparencia para el Pueblo,"* a deconcentrated organ of the Secretaría) `[verified]` (same URLs).
- Whether the new Reglamento has been published is `[not found]` — not re-checked this session; the sibling file's warning (2011 Reglamento of uncertain force) stands until someone checks the DOF index. Flag carried, not resolved.
- No new app obligations follow from any of this for S1 purposes: the law binds her as *responsable* regardless of language or format `[inferred]`. It is context for why consent paperwork (aviso de privacidad, written consent) exists, not input to section names.

## 4. Q3 — Common progress-note structures in Spanish

### 4.1 SOAP in Spanish: Subjetivo, Objetivo, Análisis (o Evaluación), Plan

- Letter by letter, per UNAM FES Iztacala teaching material: **S: Subjective (subjetivo) — O: Objective (objetivo) — A: Assessment (evaluación, análisis) — P: Plan (plan)** `[verified]` (https://www.medicinaconductual-unam-fesi.org/uploads/1/0/3/4/103420148/elaboraci%C3%B3n_de_nota_soap_y_examen_mental.pdf). The same source calls SOAP notes *notas de evolución* adaptable to psychologists' work.
- Mexican practice splits on A: Kalyo teaches **A = Análisis** (clinical hypothesis + advance vs treatment objectives) `[verified for usage, not authority]` (https://kalyo.io/notas-soap-psicologia/); GalenX teaches **A = Evaluación** (clinical hypothesis, differential, progress assessment) `[verified for usage]` (https://www.galenx.mx/blog/ejemplos-de-notas-clinicas-psicologicas-soap-dap-y-libre/). Both agree on S = Subjetivo, O = Objetivo, P = Plan.
- Content conventions per section (terminology-level summary, paraphrased, not copied): S carries the person's own report, at times with short verbatim quotes; O carries observable conduct, mental-state examination elements, and instrument scores (PHQ-9, GAD-7 named by both vendors); A carries the hypothesis/differential and progress reading; P carries the intervention performed, homework, follow-up interval and next-session date.
- NOM-004 does not impose the name SOAP: it requires *notas de evolución* with the §6.2 elements, and SOAP is the de-facto structure that carries them `[inferred]` (Kalyo states this explicitly; the inference is mine from §5.13 + §5.16).

### 4.2 Other structures in use

DAP (Datos, Análisis, Plan), BIRP, and free/narrative notes are all reported in current Mexican practice, with SOAP described as the most extended in clinical psychology `[verified for usage]` (https://kalyo.io/notas-soap-psicologia/ ; https://www.galenx.mx/blog/ejemplos-de-notas-clinicas-psicologicas-soap-dap-y-libre/). `[inferred]` For Apunta's purposes this means: ship SOAP and her own narrative format; DAP/BIRP need no dedicated support (a custom format with three free sections already covers DAP).

### 4.3 Intake / historia clínica psicológica: usual section names

No binding section list exists for psychology (that is what §5.16 says). The usual private-practice shape, aggregated from practice sources (terminology only): ficha de identificación (datos del paciente); motivo de consulta; antecedentes (personales, familiares, patológicos/no patológicos); exploración / examen del estado mental; hipótesis o impresión diagnóstica (DSM-5-TR or CIE-11 per current practice sources); plan terapéutico (enfoque, objetivos, frecuencia); consentimiento informado; then notas de evolución per session `[inferred from consistent vendor/practice usage — Kalyo https://kalyo.io/articulos/nom-004-historia-clinica-mexico.html and CONASAMA annexes https://www.conasama.salud.gob.mx/Residenciales/Guxa_expediente_res_profesional_13_09_21.pdf agree on the skeleton; individual labels vary]`. This skeleton is the source basis for the intake proposals in §5.3.
